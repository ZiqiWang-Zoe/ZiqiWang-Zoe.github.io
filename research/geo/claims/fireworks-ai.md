# Fireworks AI: claims from its own site

Copied 2026-10-19 from https://fireworks.ai/pricing. Quoted text checked against the live page.

- P1 Serverless inference is pay per token with prepaid credits ("Pay per token with pre-paid, usage-based billing"). Per-model token prices are listed in the pricing docs, not on this page.
- P2 On-demand GPUs: H100 80 GB $8.00/hour, H200 141 GB $8.00/hour, B200 180 GB $13.00/hour, billed per second.
- P3 Fine-tuning is priced per 1M training tokens (LoRA SFT from $0.50 per 1M for models up to 16B).
