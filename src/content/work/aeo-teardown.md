## Plan

1. Pick five AI infrastructure companies and write 10 to 15 questions a real buyer would ask.
2. Run every question on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews on the same day.
3. Record which sources each engine cites, which of the company's own claims survive, and when a competitor is named instead.
4. Publish the method, the full citation table, and what each company could change.

This builds directly on [L-MARS](/work/l-mars/), which studies the same source-selection step.
